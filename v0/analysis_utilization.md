# Occupancy and utilization of worship spaces

A worship space is the building a congregation gathers in; congregations sharing worship space are one space. Occupancy is congregations per space; utilization is weekly hours of worship held there (from congregations' own posted schedules), counted only where every congregation in the space has a schedule. Religious buildings only, unless stated. Placement: website gathering address where extraction found one, else the listing.

## Caveats

- Hours come from congregations' own websites and cover only spaces where every congregation posts a schedule; small and renting congregations post less, so utilization describes the better-documented spaces.
- A schedule lists services, not other uses (meetings, schools, community programs), so hours understate how much a building is used.
- Durations not posted are filled with typical lengths by tradition (analysis_services.py).
