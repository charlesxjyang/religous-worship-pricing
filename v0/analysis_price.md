# Sharing and the local price of commercial space, five cities

Congregations placed at the Overture listing address (website extraction was not run in this database). Price: assessed market value of land per square foot for retail and office lots, median within 1,000 m of each congregation (2,000 m where fewer than 10 lots lie within 1,000 m). NYC: PLUTO land assessment / 0.45; DC: OTR land value; Charlotte: county CAMA land value over parcel area; SF: assessed land value of parcels sold since 2015 (Proposition 13); Houston: HCAD market land value. Assessed values are the assessor's estimate of market value, not observed rents.

## Caveats

- Assessed land values are model estimates; NYC's class 4 land values in particular are an allocation of an income-based total. They rank places by price well, less well as levels.
- Sharing in NYC is likely understated where pairs are split across neighbouring lots (the near-miss check in the map page), and that happens more in dense, expensive areas.
- Prices and congregation density rise together; the third model holds nearby congregations fixed.
- Charlotte has only 15 planning areas to cluster on, so its own standard errors are rough.
